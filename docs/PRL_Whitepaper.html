01 · The Core Idea

Not a chatbot. Not a notes app. A second memory.

PRL continuously learns from a person's experiences, documents, habits, conversations, media, and actions — transforming scattered information into structured knowledge and long-term intelligence. Instead of merely storing information, it understands the relationships between memories, anticipates future needs, and preserves a person's knowledge for decades.

It exists to answer one fundamental problem:

"Human memory is limited, fragmented, and temporary. PRL creates a persistent and evolving memory system that grows alongside its owner throughout life."

What people lose every day

Documents
IDs, property papers, policies, certificates — misplaced.
Stories
Family history and wisdom, forgotten with a generation.
Records
Medical history and finances, scattered and fragmented.

PRL is a digital extension of human memory that never forgets and continuously organises life into meaning. The system transforms data → understanding → wisdom.

02 · Concept & Philosophy

From data, to understanding, to wisdom.

Most tools stop at storage. PRL is built as a pipeline that keeps climbing the ladder of abstraction — each layer adds meaning the layer below could not see.

Data

Raw signals: a commit, a bill, a prescription, a voice note, a photo, a chat.

Information

Signals normalised into typed, time-stamped, embedded memories.

Knowledge

Memories connected into a graph of people, projects, places & concepts.

Wisdom

Patterns, predictions, blind-spots & a model that reasons the way you do.

A first principle: never fabricate

Every PRL insight is evidence-grounded — each one carries a score, a confidence, the evidence behind it, and a plain-language reason. When the data is too thin to support a claim, the system says nothing rather than inventing one. PRL measures; it never moralises.

04 · The Memory Model

Human-like memory, in layers.

Each memory is the atomic unit of reality: a time-stamped, embedded record with its source, importance, emotion, location and the entities it involves. Memories are typed into human-like layers so retrieval can reason about what kind of memory it is.

Episodic

Personal experiences & events.

Semantic

Facts, knowledge & skills.

Social

People & interactions.

Goal

Stated intentions & aspirations.

Procedural*

Routines & how-to skill.

Emotional*

Moments that carried weight.

The Life Graph — five cognitive regions

Entities map onto the brain regions visualised in the Cognitive Core. Region intensity is data-driven — the more a region is fed, the brighter it burns.

Memory (places)   Knowledge (skills)   Goal (goals)   Social (people)   Project (projects)

* Procedural and emotional layers are on the near-term roadmap; episodic, semantic, social and goal typing are implemented.

05 · The Cognitive Engine Suite

Sixteen ways of understanding a person.

Each engine is a pure, testable core plus a thin database adapter. All are explainable — score, confidence, evidence, reason — and all refuse to speak below their data threshold.

Cognitive TwinEvidence-backed traits: learning style, persistence, curiosity, focus, attention rhythm.

06 · The Ledger · Life-Domain Cortexes

Finance. Health. Family.

Where the cognitive suite models who you are, the Ledger cortexes organise what your life contains. Each reads structured records mined from everyday text and returns an organised, evidence-backed view — and, like every PRL engine, stays silent when there is nothing to say.

Finance Cortex

  • Spend trend & top categories
  • Recurring-bill detection
  • Next-due prediction
  • Savings rate
  • Anomaly detection (robust median)

Health Cortex

  • Active medications
  • Doctor visits
  • Test-result trends
  • Refill & check-up reminders
  • record-keeping, not advice

Family Cortex

  • Relationship map
  • Preserved stories & recipes
  • Birthdays & anniversaries
  • Next-occurrence + reminders
  • Generational continuity
